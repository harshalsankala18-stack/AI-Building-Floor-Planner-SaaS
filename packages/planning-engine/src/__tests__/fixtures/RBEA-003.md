# RBEA-003 Professional Reference Fixture

## Purpose and evidence boundary

This redacted, test-only fixture records a visually audited one-sheet first-floor residential working plan. It is not consumed by generation, scoring, repair, ranking, rendering, or 3D projection. The source drawing and identifying title block are not stored.

`VERIFIED_FROM_SOURCE` means the fact is visible on the audited sheet. `ASSERTED_FROM_BRIEF` identifies a generalized planning interpretation rather than drawing text. `AMBIGUOUS` prevents a label or nearby geometry from being promoted into ownership or access certainty. `REFERENCE_ACCEPTED_BY_PRACTICE` is user-reported; it does not validate this software or any generated plan.

## Source-audited inventory

The single residential sheet contains three bedrooms; Master Bath, Attach and Common Attach bathrooms; Dressing; Utility; Family Sitout; Kitchen Cum Dining; Living; Pooja; Balcony; Entrance Lobby; Entry; Staircase; and Covered Terrace. Two road edges are visible: one at the left page edge and one angled along the lower frontage. No road width or compass mapping is inferred.

## Access and ownership topology

- Master Bedroom -> Dressing -> Master Bath is visible. The bath door is from Dressing, so no direct bedroom-to-bath door is asserted.
- The upper Bedroom and the bathroom labelled Attach have separate doors into an unlabelled access bay. That bay continues toward the Entrance Lobby side. The label is preserved as an association, but exclusive access is ambiguous.
- Common Attach opens from the common Family Sitout side. Its compound label is retained, but no private bedroom owner is invented.
- Staircase -> Entry -> Entrance Lobby -> Living forms the arrival sequence.
- Living and Family Sitout distribute toward private and service rooms without requiring transit through an unrelated bedroom.
- Kitchen Cum Dining clusters with Family Sitout; Utility and Pooja remain directly associated with that common/service zone.
- Living visibly opens to Covered Terrace through the scheduled folding door. Balcony access is left unresolved because a confident door route is not legible.

Visible opening tags support perimeter/exposure observations for bedrooms, Living, Kitchen Cum Dining, Pooja, Utility, bathrooms and Staircase. This is not a daylight, ventilation, egress, or code-compliance result.

## Generalizable planning evidence

- An irregular/angled frontage can coexist with a mostly orthogonal interior plan.
- Entry, lobby, living and family areas form a compact common hierarchy.
- Combined kitchen/dining can share a common core with family space while keeping utility and pooja legible.
- Bathroom labels alone are insufficient: door topology determines direct access, common access and ownership confidence.
- Covered terraces and balconies are intentional outdoor/semi-open program, not automatic waste.
- Perimeter-dependent openings are useful reference evidence but do not prove environmental performance.

## Project-specific observations—not global rules

The exact room count, room sizes, opening quantities, wall annotations, dual-road form, angled lower frontage, balcony and covered-terrace arrangement belong to this project. Their positions are not generator placement rules.

RBEA-002 is a three-sheet mixed-use/upper-residential reference with repeated vertical core and upper-floor coordination.
